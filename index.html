A LITTLE CARE GOES A LONG WAY

Keep your sarees beautiful for years.

01. Follow the care label
Always check the fabric-specific washing and dry-cleaning instructions before cleaning.

02. Store with care
Keep sarees clean and completely dry. Use a breathable cloth cover and avoid prolonged direct sunlight.

03. Refold periodically
Refold stored sarees from time to time to reduce stress on the same creases and zari areas.

HELPFUL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Need help choosing a saree? Here are a few things customers often ask.

How can I know the price and availability of a saree?

Send us an enquiry through the contact form or WhatsApp with the saree category or product image. Our team can confirm the current price and availability.

Do you help customers choose sarees for specific occasions?

Yes. Share your occasion, preferred colours, style and approximate budget. We can guide you towards suitable options from the available collection.

Can I visit the store?

Yes. Please contact us before visiting to confirm the store address, timings and availability of the styles you are looking for.

Do you provide delivery?

Delivery options and charges depend on the destination and order. Please contact us to confirm serviceability and estimated delivery details.

How do I care for silk or embellished sarees?

Follow the care instructions for the specific fabric. For delicate silk, zari or embellished sarees, professional dry cleaning is generally the safer option.
